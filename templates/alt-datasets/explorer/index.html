LuxAlgo Alt Data explorer

LuxAlgo Alt Data publishes the public record of US markets — congressional trades, insider filings, 13F holdings, government contracts & grants, lobbying filings, short-sale volume, committee assignments, patents, clinical trials, FDA drug-application events, CFTC positioning, Wikipedia pageviews, federal legislation, and FEC campaign-finance data — as free daily dumps. Every row is parsed from a primary source and carries a working link back to it.
