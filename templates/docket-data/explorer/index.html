Docket data explorer

Docket publishes the public record of US markets — congressional trades, insider filings, 13F holdings, government contracts & grants, lobbying, short-sale volume, committee assignments, patents, clinical trials, FDA events, and CFTC positioning — as free daily dumps. Every row is parsed from a primary source and carries a working link back to it.

This data is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 — use it for anything, no attribution required. Source and parsers: github.com/LuxAlgo/docket.
